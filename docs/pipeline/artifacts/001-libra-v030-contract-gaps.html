CIMD-4372 response examples do not validate against the Libra Gateway contract

Every example response in the CIMD-4372 ticket fails schema validation against libra-gateway-hearing-events-openapi-v0.3.0.yml — the contract the ticket states they were generated from. Six of the twelve NOWS entity names it specifies are not properties of NowsDataItems, which declares additionalProperties: false. AC1 and AC2 cannot both be satisfied as written. Update (v0.4.0): the contract has since moved to v0.4.0, which grew the resultCode enum but left NowsDataItems and every nested schema byte-identical to v0.3.0 — the findings below still hold verbatim. See section G.

A. How this was found

Step 1
Both attachments read in full
The 15-page CIMD-4372 export and the 1,539-line v0.3.0 OpenAPI document.
Step 2
AC2 checked against the schema
AC2 says response keys are the camelCased request names. Each of the 12 NowsDataItemName enum values was camelCased and looked up in NowsDataItems.properties. Six do not exist.
Step 3
Example bodies checked against the schema
The remaining six exist but are typed differently from the ticket's examples — four are scalars where the ticket shows objects.
Step 4
Result codes cross-checked
The ticket's table holds 30 short codes; the resultCode enum holds 24 values (21 distinct outcomes). The two sets disagree in both directions.
Step 5
Field labels mapped onto schema paths
Six field labels used by postable codes have no property anywhere in v0.3.0.

B. Evidence — the twelve entity names

# NowsDataItemName (AC2 camelCase) -> property of NowsDataItems in v0.3.0
"Defendant Account"               -> defendantAccount             ABSENT  (schema: defendant)
"Account History"                 -> accountHistory               ABSENT  (schema: paymentHistory/transactionHistory)
"Account Offences and Penalties"  -> accountOffencesAndPenalties   ABSENT  (schema: offences)
"Account Terms to Pay"            -> accountTermsToPay             ABSENT  (schema: terms)
"CT Account Bank Details"         -> ctAccountBankDetails          ABSENT  (schema: ctBankDetails)
"Days Before Release of Warrant"  -> daysBeforeReleaseOfWarrant    ABSENT  (schema: daysBeforeReleaseWarrant)
"Account Balance"                 -> accountBalance               present, but typed number
"Account Warrant Number"          -> accountWarrantNumber          present, but typed string ^\d{3}/\d{2}/\d{5}$
"Account Bail Amount"             -> accountBailAmount             present, but typed number
"Warrant Contact Details"         -> warrantContactDetails         present, but fields are Line1..Line5
"Account Number"                  -> accountNumber                present
"Account Date Imposed"            -> accountDateImposed           present

# NowsDataItems declares additionalProperties: false — an ABSENT key is a hard validation failure.

The ticket's own worked example for BWTD returns accountWarrantNumber: {"warrantNo": "W000012345"} and daysBeforeReleaseOfWarrant: {"days": 14}. Under v0.3.0 the first must be a string matching NNN/NN/NNNNN, and the second key does not exist at all.

E. Findings

Confirmed
AC1 and AC2 are mutually exclusive as written
AC1 requires the body to validate under additionalProperties: false; AC2 requires six keys the schema does not declare. No implementation can satisfy both.
Confirmed
AC4's formats contradict the schema in two places
Warrant No A(10) (W000012345) against pattern ^\d{3}/\d{2}/\d{5}$; Date Imposed YYYY-MM-DD against x-date-format: DD Mon YYYY.
Confirmed
AC7 relies on an undeclared header
X-Idempotency-Key appears nowhere in v0.3.0 — only X-Correlation-ID is defined.
Ruled out
AC5 needs work to suppress enforcerCode
v0.3.0's response schema declares no enforcerCode property at any level, so it cannot be emitted. AC5 is satisfied structurally.
Open
Is "Account History" one entity or two?
It camelCases to nothing; the schema offers paymentHistory and transactionHistory. Interim behaviour: emit both.
Confirmed
Are the 10 unreachable rows awaiting a later spec version? — partly, yes
The ticket notes a reference-data dependency (CIMD-4336). v0.4.0 added 6 of the 10 (ACF, AEC, FIDIC, FIDICT, FTTP, PTNV) to the resultCode enum — their field lists, already transcribed from the CIMD-4372 table, are now reachable and postable. The remaining 4 (CLAMPS, LATG, LATR, PGPAY) are still absent from the enum and still marked postable: false. See section G.
Open
Do amounts need two decimal places on the wire?
AC4 says yes, but the schema types amounts as JSON number, which carries no scale. Interim behaviour: BigDecimal scale 2.

F. Resolution adopted

The v0.3.0 schema is treated as the contract of truth. The simulator emits only properties the schema declares; AC2's camelCase rule is re-expressed as an explicit request-name → schema-property mapping held in entity-names.yaml. Recorded as ADR-002.

Rationale: CP will eventually integrate against real Libra, which will serve the published contract. A simulator matching the ticket's examples would train CP against a shape that cannot exist in production.

Action needed from the GOB integration team: either correct the CIMD-4372 examples to match v0.3.0, or publish a v0.4.0 that adopts the nested shapes the ticket assumes. Until then the nine open questions in the design spec carry stated interim behaviours, so delivery is not blocked.

G. Update — v0.4.0 adoption (2026-09-18)

The bundled contract was upgraded from v0.3.0 to v0.4.0. Diffing the two files directly: the only change is the resultCode enum, which grew from 24 to 42 values. NowsDataItems and every schema nested under it (the twelve entity components evidenced in section B) are byte-identical between the two versions.

Consequence: one open question is partly answered — 6 of the 10 rows that were "in the CIMD-4372 table, not in the enum" (ACF, AEC, FIDIC, FIDICT, FTTP, PTNV) are now in the v0.4.0 enum and postable; their field lists needed no correction, since they were already transcribed correctly from the CIMD-4372 table while unreachable. The other 4 (CLAMPS, LATG, LATR, PGPAY) remain absent from the enum and unpostable. v0.4.0 also added 15 further codes with no CIMD-4372 table row at all (mappings pending from the vendor) and dropped the CP-side spellings WC, TFOUT, WWDN in favour of their GOB aliases DW, TFOOUT, WDN (preserved in the catalogue as explicit aliases).

The core divergence recorded in this document is unchanged and remains open. All of sections B, D and E — the six absent entity names, the four retyped ones, and the six unmappable field labels — hold exactly as evidenced, because the schemas they were checked against did not change. The GOB integration team has not yet corrected the CIMD-4372 examples, nor published a contract version that adopts the ticket's nested shapes.
